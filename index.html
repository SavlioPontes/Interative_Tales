INTERACTIVE TALES

An Adventure Through Ancient Legends

HOW IT WORKS

This is a collection of interactive, text-based adventures. Read the story, listen to the narration, and make choices to shape the hero's fate. Some chapters include quizzes to test your knowledge.

Note: Each story is self-contained. You can explore them in any order.
