INTERACTIVE TALES

An Adventure Through Ancient Legends

HOW IT WORKS

This is a collection of interactive, text-based adventures. Read the story and make choices when prompted. Some chapters may include quizzes to test your understanding before proceeding.

Note: Each story is self-contained. You can explore them in any order.
